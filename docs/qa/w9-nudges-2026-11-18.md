## What ships

An hourly tick task, `learner_nudges`, sends four kinds of reminder to students. Each is an in-app notification, plus email and push where allowed:

| Kind | When | Essential |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Due soon: weekly milestone | 24h before the end of its Guam due day | No |
| Due soon: final project | 48h and 24h before its effective close time | No |
| No recent activity | 7 and 14 days without learning activity, after the learner's start, while a required lesson is open and unfinished or a redo is waiting | No |
| Help period ending | 7 days and 1 day before `support_expires_at` (skipped when access ends within a day of it) | No |
| Access ending | 14 days and 3 days before `access_expires_at` | Yes |

**Gating.** Reminders need the school `learner_nudges` switch (default **Off**, in `FeatureSwitches::DEFAULTS`) and the cohort's own opt-in. A cohort that inherits gets Off even when the school is On, the same rule as `github_app` (`FeatureSwitches::COHORT_OPT_IN`). Alumni cohorts never get reminders. A completed cohort's learners keep only the essential access notice, since they keep access until it expires. With the school switch off, the task reads one row and stops.

**Effective dates.** Milestones come from `LearnerProgress#pace`. A final-date extension shifts them, because #316 moves the plan. The final project's close time comes from #316's resolver (`SubmissionWindowContext#close_at`), extensions included. Each key holds the date it was sent for. After staff extend a deadline, the old date is never a candidate again, and the new date gets its own 48h and 24h reminders.

**Exactly once.** Each reminder is a `learner_nudges` row with a unique key (learner, event and date, threshold). That row and its notification are inserted in one transaction before any email or push. Reruns, overlapping ticks and a concurrent claim all lose the key and send nothing. Email is attempted at most once, and its result (`sent`, `failed`, `not_configured`, `no_address`) is kept on the row. Push uses `PushNotificationJob`'s existing per-provider claim on the row's own outbox columns. The task re-requests a push still unsettled after 5 minutes and drops one older than 12 hours.

**Quiet hours and the daily cap.** Nothing is sent from 21:00 to 08:00 Guam time. The first tick at or after 08:00 evaluates everything again with current dates. A reminder deferred overnight goes out then, at its tightest threshold, and is never sent for a date that moved. Each learner gets at most one reminder per Guam day: the most urgent wins (final project, then milestone, then help period, then inactivity), and the rest are reconsidered on later ticks. The access notice is the exception: the cap never holds it back, and on a day it goes out, optional reminders wait.

**Dates.** Guided terms end at 00:00 Guam on the day after the last day, so titles and bodies name the last usable day (support ending at Dec 3 00:00 reads "ends today" on Dec 2).

**Opt-out.** Learners turn optional reminders off under Profile > Notifications ("Course reminders", web and mobile). The switch shows only to learners with an active enrollment in a cohort that sends reminders. Push still follows the existing web and device push switches.

**Why access-ending is essential.** After access ends, the learner can't open lessons or their own submitted work. Missing that notice means losing what they paid for or were given, with no later chance to save their work. It's a notice about their account, not a nudge to study, so it goes even when reminders are off. The help period ending is optional: the learner keeps their lessons, and Today already shows the date.

**Staff view.** The student workspace's Learning tab lists the reminders sent to that learner, newest first. Each row shows its kind, threshold, Guam send time, email and push status, and whether the learner read it. It also says when the learner opted out or the cohort has reminders off. The panel appears only for cohorts that set the switch themselves, so a cohort that turns reminders off keeps its history visible; cohorts that never set it see no change. A push the outbox gave up on reads "Push not sent (too late)".

**Off cohorts.** Every cohort today, Lance's Cohort 3 included, behaves exactly as before. The W9.1 golden file changes only by the new `"learner_nudges": false` key, 16 times, in the feature maps. A new parity test runs the hourly tick for 21 days with the school switch On. It covers a Cohort 3–shaped program, program v2, guided, alumni, explicitly Off and paced-but-not-opted-in cohorts, all with access and support ending soon. Result: no rows and no notifications, profile and staff payloads carry no reminder flags, and the task's query count doesn't change when those cohorts exist.

**Bounded work.** Enrollments are read in id batches of 200 within a 5-minute budget, like `ScheduledAnnouncementPublisher`. When the budget runs out, the task raises `BacklogRemaining` and records the last enrollment checked (`resume_after`, kept in the task's `last_result`). The next run starts after it and wraps round, so learners with higher ids are never starved. Emails are spaced 0.6 s apart to stay under Resend's per-second limit. A learner whose evaluation raises is logged and skipped; the others still get theirs, and the task reports failure so the next tick retries. The only lock is the tick's existing transaction-level advisory lock.

## Independent review

A fresh-context review of the uncommitted diff (read-only, given the code and the brief's rules) found no P1. It confirmed the claim and dedupe path, the push claim races, the PgBouncer and lock usage, the gating and query costs, the threshold math, the controller scoping and the OpenAPI shapes. Fixed from its findings, each with a test:

- A run cut short by the budget restarted at id 0 every time, so high-id learners could be starved. It now resumes and wraps.
- Midnight-ending guided terms read one day late. Copy now names the last usable day.
- Completed cohorts stopped receiving the essential access notice. They now get only that.
- Email statuses were written after the whole batch. They're now written per reminder.
- An expired push showed as attempted. It now shows "not sent".
- The staff panel vanished when a cohort turned reminders off. It now stays for cohorts that set the switch.
- Emails went out back to back. They're now paced against Resend's rate limit.

Left as residuals (below): inactivity counted across a pause, and lesson or module extensions not moving soft milestones.

## Decisions to confirm and residuals

- **Copy.** The design asks Leon to approve each template. The current wording is in `api/app/services/learner_nudges.rb` and the email in `NotificationEmailService#learner_nudge_html`.
- **Thresholds.** Milestones get only the 24h reminder (they're weekly and soft); the final project gets 48h and 24h. In practice, with quiet hours, these land at 08:00 the day before and 08:00 on the due day.
- **Not built from the design's longer list:** "one milestone behind" as its own reminder, a two-a-week cap, and the staff review-SLA alerts (also 9.4b). The brief scoped this slice to the learner reminders above.
- **Staff plans in cohorts without personal pace** have milestones but no pace measure, so they get no milestone reminders. Their final project still gets one if it has a close time.
- **Email and push are at most once.** If the process dies after the claim commits, that reminder's email is lost. A lost push is recovered by the outbox within 12 hours.
- **Pauses.** Resuming clears `paused_at`, and nothing records when the learner came back. A learner resumed after a long pause can get an inactivity reminder the next morning that counts the paused weeks. Fixing it needs a stored resume time.
- **Lesson and module extensions** change the final project's close time when they apply to it, but don't move weekly milestones; only a final-date extension shifts the plan (#316). Milestones are soft for learner-pace plans, so the effect is a reminder about a week the learner has extra time on.
- **Resend limits.** Emails are spaced 0.6 s apart. A 429 is still recorded as `failed` and not retried, because the reminder is already delivered in-app.
- Not run: WebKit or mobile Safari (unsupported on this Mac mini), native device push, real email delivery.
