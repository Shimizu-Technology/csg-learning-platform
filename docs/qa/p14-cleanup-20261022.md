## PR #203 review-fix round 2 — 2026-10-22

Starting state: local `fix/p14-cleanup` and `origin/fix/p14-cleanup` both verified at `8eaff41cfb749de6f4613fc8cd75f4613a0fbf8d`, with `origin/main` at `d5406f3fac2e863e092cf18148e7a0151526de71`. This round addresses the lead's two remaining native cached-access findings. The inherited product brief still applies: students use cached learning content on phones, access ends at the enrollment deadline, and only successful online access validation can resume writes.

Design:

- `useCohort` derives saved cohort visibility and selection from enrollment status, `access_expires_at`, and `Date.now()` on every consumer render. No stored `stale` flag grants access. One next-deadline timer prompts all mounted consumers to render, then re-arms for the earliest future deadline across all remaining cached cohorts. Foreground return also prompts a render before waiting for validation.
- Selection updates its own ref and persisted choice. It never increments the validation generation. Concurrent refresh/reconnect calls share one in-flight validation; success applies fresh access, resolves the latest choice against that access, and clears the API write block. Account changes/unmount still invalidate old requests.
- Failed validation retries after 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, then 30 seconds, capped at 30 seconds between attempts. Recovery does not require another connectivity/foreground event. Success clears the failure count and cancels retry monitoring. The failure count also supplies the unresolved-access state, avoiding duplicate flags.
