# CSG Connect 1.0.0 (29): cohort workspaces

Confirm TestFlight shows **1.0.0 (29)** before testing. Use authorized staff and student accounts. A dedicated test course and bootcamp are best; keep private student content out of defect reports.

1. As an admin, switch between **All cohorts**, a bootcamp, and a short course. Open each cohort home. Check the roster's joined and not yet opened counts, work to grade, help requests, announcements, and schedule. Confirm each count and link stays with the selected cohort.
2. Assign an instructor to one cohort. Sign in as that instructor and confirm only assigned cohort workspaces, messages, students, grading, and support appear. Revoke the assignment and confirm access disappears after refresh.
3. In a cohort, create or edit a live class or office-hours session in Guam time. Check that the upcoming session and meeting link appear on the student's cohort home. Open class messages and post a test announcement.
4. As a student enrolled in two cohorts, switch between them. Confirm Today, Learn, messages, recordings, resources, and the cohort home follow the selected cohort. Check that a completed cohort is available as read-only history.
5. If two cohorts share curriculum, complete a learning step and submit test work in one. Switch to the other and verify its progress and submissions remain separate. Return to the first and confirm the original evidence is still there.
6. Repeat the cohort home and switcher checks on desktop or mobile web at phone width. Check that Schedule and Communication are visible in the cohort navigation without a hidden horizontal swipe.

Report the device, iOS version, role, cohort, exact steps, and whether a defect also occurs on web. Complete the broader [physical TestFlight acceptance checklist](README.md#physical-testflight-acceptance) before any public App Review submission.
