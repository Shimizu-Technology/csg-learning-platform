Use this copy as the English (United States) default listing. Confirm every statement against the submitted build before publishing.

## Listing fields

**App name**

CSG Connect

**Short description**

Learn, message, submit work, and stay connected with Code School of Guam.

**Full description**

CSG Connect is the mobile learning hub for invited Code School of Guam students, instructors, and administrators.

Keep your class day in one place:

- See what is due, unlocked, and coming next
- Read lessons and access class resources
- Submit coursework and review grades and instructor feedback
- Watch class recordings and continue where you left off
- Follow announcements and notifications
- Join cohort channels and direct conversations
- Ask for help and stay connected with instructors
- Give staff a focused view of grading and student-support needs

Access is limited to authorized Code School of Guam accounts. Features and content depend on your role, cohort, and current enrollment.

CSG Connect is operated by Shimizu Technology for Code School of Guam.
