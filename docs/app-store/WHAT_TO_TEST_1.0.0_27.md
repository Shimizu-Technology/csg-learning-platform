# What to Test — CSG Connect 1.0.0 (27)

Confirm TestFlight shows build `1.0.0 (27)` before testing. Use authorized staff and student accounts with a dedicated test cohort and test content. Activity history begins with this release; earlier events are not backfilled. Keep private student content out of defect reports.

1. On iPhone, open **You → Your activity history**. Confirm one sign-in appears for a fresh account session, and changing tabs or reopening the app does not create repeated sign-in entries. Try **All**, **Sign-ins**, **Learning**, and **Work**, then pull to refresh and load earlier entries if available.
2. As a student, complete and reopen a learning step, play a supported embedded YouTube or Vimeo lesson video, and submit or edit test work. Check that successful writes appear in history. Video entries should say **Player reported progress; this does not verify attention.** Periodic playback updates should not flood the timeline.
3. As staff, open a student's page and choose **Activity history**. Confirm that the student's sign-ins, learning, and work appear, and that a grade records the staff member as the actor. Check long titles, scrolling, filters, empty states, and back navigation on a compact iPhone.
4. On desktop, check the student's **Activity** tab, an administrator's **Team → Activity** view, and each member's own **Profile → Activity history**. Repeat at a narrow browser width. Verify records remain associated with the correct student and that the timeline is readable.
5. Verify role boundaries with separate accounts: a student cannot open another member's history; an instructor cannot open an administrator's history; an administrator can inspect staff history. Do not use private learner data for screenshots or bug reports.

Complete the broader [physical TestFlight acceptance checklist](README.md#physical-testflight-acceptance) before any public App Review submission. If a step fails, record device, iOS version, role, cohort, exact steps, and whether it also occurs on desktop.
