Platform redesign: mockups for review

Thirteen clickable mockups for the redesign waves W3, W5, W7 and W8, built on the proposed light and dark tokens. Nothing here is built yet. Please review before any of it ships; the written spec (glossary, nav model, patterns, motion, tokens) is in README.md next to this page.

How to use these

Light / Dark / System at the top of every mockup. The choice is remembered across pages.
Desktop / Phone switches the frame between 1280px and 390px. Layouts respond to the frame, not your window.
Variants (when shown) switch personas or states, like "Behind · Blair" or "After submit".
Notes lists what changed and the backlog IDs it covers. Sidebar and buttons link between mockups.
